Notes on Hayek

Major ideas in Hayek:

1) Hayek was writing in the mid-20th century.

2) This paper is part of the socialist calculation debate.

a) Mises and Hayek versus Lange and Lerner.

b) Can socialism rationally allocate resources?

3) Hayek: If one mind could know all preferences and all means to achieve them, the problem would be solved.

a) The "data" is never given to one mind.

4) Planning

a) Marxists sought to "rationalize" the economy by making it run according to a single, master plan.

b) Is it to be done centrally or not?

c) Planning today often means central planning.

5) Scientific knowledge versus practical knowledge

a) Particular circumstances of time and place

b) On the job learning

c) Knowing details of idle machines, workers personalities, alternate routes, etc.

6) The importance of change

a) Aggregate statistics obscure details.

b) Job losses are not just any jobs.

c) Number of construction machines does not take account of their location.

7) How can the individual grasp the context of his/her decision?

8) Prices!

a) Price system is a "marvel."

b) How prices convey knowledge of relative scarcity.

c) We advance by what we don't need to think about.

9) Today even socialists recognize the importance of the work of Hayek and Mises.
