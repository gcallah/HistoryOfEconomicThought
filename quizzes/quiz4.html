Pseudo-Quiz 4

History of Economic Thought: Pseudo-Quiz 4

Name: ____________________________________________________

1) In Keynes’s model, a recession starts when:

a) Unemployment rises more than 2%.

b) Investors lose their confidence.

c) The central bank sets the interest rate too high.

d) The structure of production is distorted.

2) In Hayek’s model, the roots of a recession lie in:

a) Animal spirits.

b) The paradox of thrift.

c) Credit expansion by the central bank.

d) The paradox of thrift.

3) In Keynes’s model:

a) Consumption is regarded as stable but investment will vary erratically.

b) Investment is regarded as stable but consumption will vary erratically.

c) It is government expenditures that throw the economy out of wack.

d) The best policy is one of laissez-faire.

4) Two major political leaders influenced by Hayek were:

a) Joseph Stalin and Mao Zedong

b) Saddam Hussein and Fidel Castro

c) Benito Mussolini and Haile Selassie.

d) Margaret Thatcher and Ronald Reagan.

5) The Keynesian “paradox of thrift” is that:

a) It’s really the big spenders who are most thrifty.

b) If everyone tries to save more, they will wind up earning less.

c) It’s really thriftiness that is next to godliness, not cleanliness.

d) When someone looks for cheap items but winds up buying something expensive.

6) The “Hayekian triangle” is based on:

a) Adam Smith’s theory of the division of labor.

b) Karl Marx’s theory of exploitation.

c) Thomas Malthus’s theory of population growth.

d) Carl Menger’s theory of different orders of capital goods.

7) A key difference between Hayek’s model and Keynes’s is that:

a) In Hayek’s model, capital is viewed as having a complex structure.

b) Keynes does not include wages as income.

c) Hayek believes that setting interest rates at an arbitrary level will not affect the economy.

d) Keynes thought the economy was always in an optimal equilibrium.

8) For Keynes, an important antidote to recession was:

a) Socialization of the means of production.

b) Deficit reduction.

c) Government spending.

d) High tariffs.

9) Hayek thought that the prevailing rate of interest, if left to market forces, would:

a) Balance savings and investment.

b) Get all wack on us.

c) Create major unemployment.

d) Would continually rise.

10) According to the documentary The Commanding Heights, one factor leading people to question Keynes’s theory in the 1970s was:

a) Stagflation.

b) Richard Nixon.

c) The existence of the BeeGees.

d) The ending of the Vietnam War.
