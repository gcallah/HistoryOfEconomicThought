History of Economic Thought: Quiz 3

Name: ____________________________________________________

1) The three founders of marginalism are generally considered to be:

a) Menger, Jevons, and Walras

b) Smith, Ricardo, and Mill

c) Marx, Engels, and Lenin

d) Keynes, Hayek, and Krugman

2) Menger held that, for something to be an economic good, it must be:

a) pretty

b) expensive

c) scarce

d) available freely

3) Menger held that a good of a higher order derives its value from:

a) The labor that went into it.

b) The greed of capitalists.

c) The global economy.

d) The consumer good(s) it helps to produce.

4) A definite example of a higher order good would be:

a) a loaf of bread

b) an ice cream cone

c) a furnace for smelting iron

d) an MP3 from a really good band

5) One sharp difference between Menger and most modern neoclassical economists is:

a) Menger did not understand marginal utility

b) most modern neoclassicals believe in the labor theory of value

c) most modern neoclassicals believe in the increasing misery of the proletariat, while Menger thought workers were getting happier

d) most modern neoclassicals see prices and quantities as simultaneously determined, while Menger saw economic process as causal and taking place over time

6) Menger agreed with Adam Smith on the importance of the division of labour, but thought Smith neglected the importance of:

a) the deepening of the capital structure

b) land rents

c) the rate of interest on loans

d) the creation of large monopolies

7) Menger resolved the diamond-water paradox by:

a) showing how DeBeers had monopolized the diamond market, driving the price artificially high

b) pointing out that it is the value of the marginal diamond or unit of water that is under consideration in any real choice

c) marshaling extensive medical evidence to show that water is not really very good for you

d) he interviewed many rich people to see why they liked diamonds so much

8) Menger held that value is:

a) something contained in goods themselves

b) due to the labour put into the good

c) a mystery

d) subjective and based on personal evaluation

a) Gasoline, fireworks, and tacos
