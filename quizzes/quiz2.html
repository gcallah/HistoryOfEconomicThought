History of Economic Thought: Quiz 2

Name: ____________________________________________________

1) Marx thought that capitalism:

a) was a mistake

b) was an unadulterated evil

c) never had to be adopted

d) was a necessary historical phase

2) Marx often collaborated with:

a) Adam Smith

b) Ludwig Feurbach

c) Friedrich Engels

d) GWF Hegel

3) Marx thought he corrected Hegel by making Hegel’s theory:

a) materialistic

b) idealistic

c) dualistic

d) dialectical

4) Marx thought that capitalism was:

a) an advance on feudalism

b) a setback from the more egalitarian feudalistic society

c) feudalism under another name

d) the stage of development before feudalism

5) Socialism would be characterized by:

a) laborers earning the entire value of the product they produced

b) the dictatorship of the proletariat

c) the disappearance of the state

d) high earnings for business owners

6) Marx thought that:

a) there was no need for a return to capital

b) capital was not even a real economic category

c) capital would be done away with under socialism

d) capitalists would be unnecessary in the socialist economy

7) Marx thought that:

a) While economics sets the ground of people’s beliefs, it does not control them

b) People believe whatever their economic position leads them to believe

c) Ideas entirely control economic circumstances

d) Ideas and material conditions are two entirely separate realms with no basic connection

8) Marx thought that crises are inherent in capitalism because:

a) Workers will resist being exploited

b) The workers get paid too little to buy all of the output of capitalist production

c) The whole system is rotten

d) Producers cannot accurately predict consumer demand or the output of other producers

9) “The increasing misery of the proletariat” means chiefly:

a) The plight of the workers will be driven to starvation conditions.

b) Employees may lose their health care benefits.

c) Capitalists will not be able to maintain their profits.

d) The workers’ status will decline socially, even of they might make material gains.

10) Marx and Engels:

a) Thought democracy might be a good way to achieve socialism.

b) Felt democracy could only set the workers back.

c) Believed in dictatorship, not democracy.

d) Only believed in violent revolution.
